## Share

In the Localians sidebar:

1. Pick your running app (or **Enter a port…**).
2. Check the **Project** — Localians picks the one that matches your app, or offers to create one.
3. Optional: open **Options** to make it **Password protected** or choose how long the link works.
4. Click **Share App**.

The HTTPS preview link appears under **Active Previews** and is copied for you. Share another app the same way — each
preview runs on its own.
