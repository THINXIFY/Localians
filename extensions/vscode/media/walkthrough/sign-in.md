## Sign in once

Click **Sign In to Localians** in the Localians sidebar (or the button on the left).

1. Your browser opens the Localians approval page.
2. Check that it shows the same one-time code as VS Code, and click **Authorize**.
3. VS Code updates by itself. Nothing to copy or paste.

Your sign-in is kept in VS Code's secure storage and works until you sign out. You can revoke it any time in the
Localians dashboard under **Settings → CLI**.
